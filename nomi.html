About our firm
The design draft, or so to speak, goal for our team was to create a wonderful and eco-friendly solution that can be used in lounges, health care, and corporate environments. Preferably a family of chairs in which the modular allows for changes over the years.

Our team engineered a ergonomic one-piece shell made of the revolutionary composite polymer. Premium builds offer high stiffness, anti-static and an excellent resistance against UV rays and detergents. The goal is to combine one single shell with a grand variety of frames, interchangeable components and a removable cover - characteristics which have elevated NOMI to a cutting-edge concept. Both modular and circular.

The centralized assembly point of the shell allows to combine it with an endless variety of frames and thus the Nomi program is in a continuing process of change. The Nomi collection started with the Classic, Work, Project, Wood, Comfort and Design models. In the past few years the Work+ and Stool Nomi models have been added in order to meet end-user demands.

The 3D-formed deep contoured shell offers a high level of luxury due to its engineered-in, but limited flexibility. This allows the shell to flex and support your body as you move.
