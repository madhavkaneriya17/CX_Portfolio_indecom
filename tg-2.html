TG-2

:

Multi-setting. Robust and sleek.

inquire about this model

The TG-2 chair is strategic and suitable for various projects. Its stackable, four-legged frame is perfect for premium and conference seating, and the seats can be interlinked if desired. Additionally, the TG-2 provides strength as a task chair and is suitable for classroom or university setups. With its ergonomic curves, it ensures user satisfaction in different configurations. Notably, the TG-2 is designed for easy assembly without screws and offers exceptional endurance.
