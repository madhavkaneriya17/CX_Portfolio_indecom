Born in the rich woodlands of Black Forest, Germany, Eric Vance found his own path back to the Netherlands with his family in the 1980s. After immersing himself in the study of ergonomics, he was irresistibly drawn towards the everlasting world of armchair crafting. The ensuing journey saw him create an impressive array of customized chairs for COMFY Furniture Company from the mid-1990s until 2019.

West Java, Indonesia

2019s

The year 2019 marked a pivotal moment in the Vance legacy when Eric and Lukas came together to found LuxeSeat Studio. Starting in Munich, but soon finding factories across the world and establishing themselves as international designers of armchairs.

Black Forest, Germany

2026s

Over the years, we've crafted luxury exclusive seating series that have graced major luxury spaces of the world, building a trusted network of showrooms. We hold supply frameworks crossing oceans - Europe, Asia, and America - all a testament to our ongoing dedication to deliver the gold of comfort to the spaces.

The mission was and still is to create aesthetic and ergonomically chairs which combine striking durability with refinement.

Our craft philosophy grows from the human master's power to create. Crafted by rich materials, ergonomics, luxury trends, or pure craftsmanship, we believe in the client's delight of modernizing spaces. We carefully select artisans and partners based on the premium and luxury standards to deliver the absolute premium.

AAa Our artisans and partners display a expertise that sets them apart, with a proven dedication to turning their concepts to reality. The design journey opens with raw creation and unfolds elegantly. Through patterns and collaboration, chairs are perfected, always with flawless precision.

Photo: Studio Spazio

Once the outline aligns, prototypes take shape, and a team is formed to combine the artisan's creativity with structural expertise. Meticulous drawings pave the way for implementation, exploring different seating parts and possibilities.

Photo: Atelier Materia

Our artisans work closely together with certified engineers in order to incorporate the latest mechanisms and know-how and thus be able to build deluxe lounge chairs.
